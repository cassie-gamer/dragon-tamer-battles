# Dragon Tamer Battles 🐉🔥

Choose your dragon and become the Dragon Master!

## The Story

Pick your dragon:
- **Night Fury** (like Toothless - black, fast, plasma blasts!)
- **Light Fury** (white, glowing, beautiful!)

Then **tame** your dragon — feed it fish, pet it, earn its trust!

Then **battle** through 10 dragon battles:
- Battles 1-3: **Weak** dragons (3 hits each)
- Battles 4-5: **Strong** dragons (6 hits each)
- Battles 6-8: **SO HARD** dragons (10 hits each)
- Battles 9-10: **BOSS** dragons (15 hits each - two bosses!)

Defeat all 10 including the two bosses to become the **Dragon Master**!

## Unity Setup

1. `DragonChoice.cs` - two buttons: "Night Fury" and "Light Fury"
2. `DragonTame.cs` - "Feed Fish" and "Pet" buttons, trust meter UI
3. `BattleManager.cs` - battle progression 1-10 with difficulty tiers
4. Hook up the UI text for battle info and trust meter
